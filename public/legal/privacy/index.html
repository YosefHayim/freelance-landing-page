Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Current status: pre-launch. The JTS WhatsApp service has not been built or opened to customers. We are working through Meta verification. This policy describes the current stage, not a live messaging service.

Who is responsible

Joseph Tech Solutions, operated by Joseph Sabag in Israel, is responsible for the JTS Meta app (app ID 1747364699947452) and inquiries about it. Contact yosefisabag@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. This notice covers JTS and these information pages; other portfolio products have their own functionality and notices.

Information handled at this stage

  • Inquiries and support: if you email us, we receive your email address, name if provided, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond, handle your request, and keep a record of that correspondence.
  • Website delivery: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical request information such as IP address, requested page, browser information, and time of access to deliver and protect these pages. Cloudflare also provides website usage and performance measurements.
  • WhatsApp customer information: there is currently no production JTS integration collecting customers' WhatsApp contacts, messages, or media. Registering the JTS app with Meta does not mean that a customer messaging service is already running.

Sharing and service providers

Cloudflare provides website hosting, and our email provider processes correspondence sent to the contact address above. Meta separately processes information submitted through its own verification and developer services under its policies. Information may also need to be disclosed when required by applicable law. JTS is not currently sending production WhatsApp customer messages to an AI provider, because that service does not yet exist.

Retention and deletion

We keep correspondence for as long as it is reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, an ongoing business relationship, or applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information held by us by emailing the address above. We will verify that you are entitled to make the request and explain the outcome, including any information that must be retained and why.

See our data deletion instructions for the information to include and how to follow up. Deleting information held by JTS does not delete records independently held by Meta, your email provider, or another business.

Security and privacy requests

These pages are served over HTTPS. Do not send passwords, access tokens, app secrets, or identity documents by email. If verification of a request requires additional information, we will discuss an appropriate way to provide it. Security concerns can be reported to yosefisabag@gmail.com.

Before the WhatsApp service launches

We will update this policy before enabling customer WhatsApp data processing. The updated notice will identify the actual information collected, purposes, service providers and any AI processing, retention arrangements, and customer controls. Any required permissions and customer agreements must be in place before that processing begins.

Changes to this notice will be published here with an updated effective date. A privacy policy or completed app-settings form is not a claim that Meta has approved JTS.
