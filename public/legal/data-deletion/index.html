Request data deletion

Effective October 4, 2026 · JTS app ID 1747364699947452

The JTS WhatsApp service is not live and does not currently collect production customer contacts or messages. You can still request deletion of inquiries or other information you have sent us.

Send a request

  1. Email yosefisabag@gmail.com with the subject JTS data deletion request.
  2. Use the email address you contacted us from when possible. Include your name or business name and describe the information or correspondence you want deleted. If relevant, include your JTS-related Meta business or app-scoped user ID, if you know it.
  3. Do not include passwords, access tokens, app secrets, identity documents, or unrelated customer message contents.
Email a deletion request

What happens next

Joseph Sabag will review the request, verify your authority to make it, and identify any matching information under JTS's control. If more information is needed to locate records or verify the request, we will reply to explain what is needed.

We will delete applicable information under our control and confirm the result by email. If information must be retained for a legal obligation or cannot be deleted for another applicable reason, we will explain that reason. If JTS holds no matching information, we will tell you.

Follow up or correct a request

Reply to the same email thread to ask for status, clarify the scope, or correct a request. We do not currently provide an automated deletion dashboard or a WhatsApp account deletion feature.

Information held elsewhere

A request to JTS does not delete your Meta or WhatsApp account, or information independently held by another business you contacted. For those records, contact Meta, WhatsApp, or the relevant business directly. Our privacy policy explains JTS's current scope.
